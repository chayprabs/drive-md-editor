Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 30, 2026

MarkDrive is a Chrome extension for editing Markdown files in Google Drive. This policy describes what data the extension handles and how it is used.

Data MarkDrive handles

  • Markdown and text files you open or save through Google Drive.
  • File metadata (names, folders, modified times) and recent files you open in MarkDrive.
  • Images you paste or upload to a Drive folder when you choose to insert them.
  • Local settings (theme, autosave, Vim mode, onboarding) stored in Chrome extension storage.
  • Offline save drafts queued locally until sync succeeds.

How data is used

File content is sent to Google Drive APIs only when you request an action: open, save, create, rename, trash, or upload. MarkDrive does not sell data, serve ads, or send document content to analytics services.

Your control

Revoke Google account access from your Google Account permissions page. Remove local data by uninstalling the extension or clearing its storage in Chrome.
